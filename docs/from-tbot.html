What changes for you, coming from TBot 3.6

What changes for you

  • For TBot 3.6 users: what the import does, what to check, and what you will notice.
  • TGoat imports your settings.json on the first start. Your old files are never changed.
  • A value still at TBot's old default takes TGoat's new default. config/import-report.txt lists each one.
  • AutoFarm and Discovery start from empty data. Expect a busy first day.
  • Every difference, row by row: Every change.

After the import

  1. Open config/import-report.txt. It lists what the import dropped.
  2. Check that your file says General.SlotPriorityLevel.AutoColonize, not Colonize.
  3. If your old file sets Expeditions.MinPrimaryToSend, it now takes effect. Check the value.
  4. Values equal to TBot's old defaults take TGoat's new defaults; the report lists them under "Moved or transformed". Write a value again to keep it.
  5. Keep a session token at hand for the first login.
  6. Expect a busy first day: AutoFarm starts from an empty database.

What changes most

  • Your old values stay, except TBot's old defaults: they take TGoat's new defaults, and the report says so. The import
  • The Defender checks every 1 to 5 minutes and keeps watching in sleep mode. Defender
  • The Brain builds mines by payback over 60 days and picks the cheapest energy. Brain
  • Expeditions have no limit per origin and leave in waves. Expeditions
  • No fleet delivers resources to a celestial an attack is heading for. Fleet sends
  • AutoFarm never attacks a player back from inactivity. AutoFarm
  • AutoFarm sends at most 20 probes and ranks targets by profit per hour. AutoFarm
  • Bedtime and wake-up shift up to 15 minutes a day. Sleep mode
  • Game requests are spaced 1.5 to 8 seconds, so logs look slower. Game requests
  • The expedition escort is automatic. Removed settings
  • Only your own Telegram chat can command the bot. Telegram and Discord

The import

It runs on first start without a config/ folder, or with tgoat import. Each instance keeps its data folder, so device and cookies carry over. What it does:

  • Read only. Old files are read once. If the old settings.json changes later, run tgoat import --again.
  • Kept: every setting that differs from the new default, with the official spelling (autofarm becomes AutoFarm).
  • Reset: a value equal to TBot's old default takes TGoat's new default, with a report line. AutoHarvest.MinimumResourcesOwnDF is kept and flagged instead. This includes the lifeform research caps: a MaxTechs31 to MaxTechs36 at 0 ("never research") in TBot's template becomes TGoat's 10, like every template value. The report lists each reset. To keep a 0, set it again after the import.
  • Not reset: half of a check interval, when resetting it would leave a fixed pause, and anything in a profile.
  • Converted, Colonize: AutoColonize.Abandon.MinFields goes into each target zone when abandon was on. An active AutoColonize.IntensiveResearch becomes AutoColonize.ParallelTries.
  • Converted, defences: your Brain.AutoDefence.DefenceToReach quantities become Brain.AutoDefence.Ratio and stay as maxima. TBot's sample quantities give TGoat's mix.
  • Moved: account, proxy and device go to the top of the instance file. Passwords, proxy login, captcha key, Discord webhook and Telegram token go to secrets.json.
  • Renamed: Brain.Transports.DeutToLeave becomes Brain.Transports.DeutToLeaveOnMoons.
  • Sample device replaced. TBot's sample values are drawn again and the old device folder is set aside. Gameforge sees a new device: expect a new login, possibly a captcha. The session token avoids it.
  • Dropped: removed settings, unknown keys, and account data or secrets found in a profile. Each is listed in config/import-report.txt.
  • Safety check. If the effective settings differ from what the old files gave, nothing is written and the report is shown.

To use a new default for a value you chose, use "reset to default" in the web interface, or /settings set <setting> default on Telegram.

Feature by feature

What a TBot user notices first, in a line or two per feature. Each name opens the full comparison on Every change.

  • Defender. Checks every 1 to 5 minutes, and keeps watching in sleep mode and while you play by hand (it then only alerts). When every fleet slot is taken, it frees one for the fleet-save. If it cannot read the attacks, it warns you, and again every 15 minutes.
  • Sleep mode. Bedtime and wake-up shift by up to 15 minutes a day. While sleep waits for fleets, no new fleet leaves. The wake-up reuses the saved session, and features start again one by one. A night round of expeditions and breaks during the day are options, off by default.
  • Human pace and play mode. Queues restart after a human delay, not 5 to 15 seconds after each build. One switch, Human (default), RAGE or Custom, sets how fast TGoat plays.
  • Game requests. 1.5 to 8 seconds between requests, with a short break every 30 to 70, so the logs look slower. The Defender and your own pages go first. Far fewer requests overall: starting the Brain, 104 reads become 47.
  • AutoFarm. Never attacks a player back from inactivity, attacks on a report at most 2 hours old, and ranks targets by profit per hour. It sends the fewest probes that show research (20 at most), simulates the battle against any defended target, and counts the real loot and losses. It can propose combat ships for targets it turns down.
  • Brain: buildings and research. Mines by payback over 60 days, energy from the cheapest source, facilities only when they serve, research by its value to the account. A build waiting for resources keeps them from repatriation.
  • Brain: lifeforms. Buildings and research chosen by payback, with no start level. When a research slot opens, TGoat chooses its technology, the guaranteed one by default.
  • Repatriation, cargo ships, defences, offer. Repatriation ships only the surplus, once it reaches a threshold, and leaves the fleet-save fuel. Defences come from a budget of 24 hours of production, split by a ratio. The offer of the day is bought once, under a price.
  • Jump gate. It jumps now (threshold 1,000 points), waits for the recharge, and never jumps to or from a moon under attack.
  • Expeditions. No limit per origin, waves at a human pace, cargo for 75% of the largest find, results read from the game's messages. TGoat can propose the ships your expeditions lack.
  • Harvest. A field whose fuel costs more than 10% of what it brings is left. Your fields are read 20 to 90 seconds after an attack, and position 16 where your expeditions went.
  • Colonization. One fields threshold per zone, zones filled in list order, at most 10 failed colonies per zone, and an abandon 3 to 20 minutes after the colony is seen. With session-token login, abandon counts as off.
  • Discovery. Explores nearest first, within 5% of the account's production a day, paid from surpluses. It can settle a lifeform on each planet that has none (off by default).
  • Directives. TGoat can collect the rewards of finished missions and chapters (off by default). TBot never did.
  • Fleet sends and slots. No fleet lands on one of your celestials just before an attack hits it. Each send leaves the deuterium for a slow fleet-save. A higher slot rank keeps only what its next pass sends.
  • Calculations. Fuel, cargo, production and costs now match the game, so the Brain and AutoFarm choose differently. See also Flight calculations.
  • Settings, start and stop. A settings change restarts only the features concerned. /stopX and /startX hold until you choose "Follow the setting". A crash in one feature no longer stops the instance.
  • Telegram and Discord. Only your own chat commands the bot. Notifications name their feature and never hold a feature up. You can run a Discord bot of your own.
  • Logs. A shorter console line with a colour per feature. Logging.Style brings back TBot's line.
  • Setup assistant. New: a few questions, then settings that fit your account as it is now, each theme applied with one click.
  • Device and network. Each account gets its own plausible PC, and TGoat connects with that browser's network fingerprint. TBot's sample device is replaced once: expect a new login.
