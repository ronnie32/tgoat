Beta software (0.1.0-beta.2). Expect bugs. Using a bot breaks OGame's rules and can get your account banned. Start with every feature off and try it on a secondary account first. Back up config/ and data/ before each update. Read the warnings before you start.

Known limits

Known limits

  • This page lists what TGoat does not do yet, or does only in part, and what real play has not confirmed.
  • For two-factor authentication or the gf-pow-captcha, sign in with your browser.
  • The web interface has no login, and secrets.json is plain text: keep both on your own computer.
  • A sleep you asked for, a planned ghost sleep, a break and pending Discord confirmations end when TGoat restarts.
  • If something on the list to check misbehaves for you, report it.
  • Two-factor authentication: there is no setting for the secret yet. Sign in with your browser, where you pass the two-factor step yourself.
  • gf-pow-captcha is not solved by TGoat and will not be. Sign in with your browser instead.
  • Sign in with your browser is built for Windows, macOS and Linux, with Chrome, Edge, Brave, Chromium, Opera and Firefox 128 or later. Checked live on Windows with Edge, Chrome and Firefox (November 2026). Brave, Chromium, Opera, macOS and Linux are built and tested with fakes, not yet checked live. On macOS, if TGoat crashes during a sign-in, the browser window can stay open until the next sign-in or start, which closes it.
  • The sign-in window needs a screen on the computer that runs TGoat, and while it is open, another user logged in to that computer could read the session token. Use it on a computer only you use. At most 8 sign-ins run at once.
  • The fleet-save neither escapes through a jump gate nor sends an expedition as a last resort yet: Defender.Autofleet.UseJumpGate and ExpeditionAsLastResort are not used yet, whatever their value.
  • The night watch on alert (Defender.NightWatch, the default) checks only after a game message, usually the attacker's spy report. An attack sent without one, or more than about 15 minutes after it, is not seen until the wake-up. Always checks all night, at the cost of activity on one planet.
  • When the server time cannot be read, the Defender's fleet-save leaves at once, for at least an hour. A last look at an empty planet already planned is then made on your computer's clock until the server time comes back, and a recall already set is not moved for a new attack: keep that clock right.
  • The web interface has no login, yet it handles passwords. It listens on localhost only by default. Keep it that way unless you protect it yourself.
  • secrets.json is plain text. Only you can read it on Linux and macOS. On Windows nothing restricts it, so keep the folder private.
  • Secret history: only the previous version is kept, in secrets.json.bak. A password changed in two places at once is not flagged as a conflict, so change it in one place at a time.
  • The saved session is not tied to its account. If you change the e-mail without a new token, the old session is dropped only when the new account has no player in the universe. Give a new token with every e-mail change.
  • Firefox and Safari devices are imitated only approximately, so new devices are never drawn with them. A device file that says Firefox or Safari keeps its browser and logs a warning. To switch, set Credentials.DeviceConf.Browser to Chrome or Edge, then remove the device folder.
  • Profiles have no schedule (such as "night from 23:00 to 07:00") and no consistency rules between settings. Check yourself that Min is not above Max.
  • Turning a feature on or off by hand is not undone if you write its Active setting while TGoat is stopped: TGoat cannot tell, and keeps your choice until you click Follow the setting.
  • Manual mode ends 30 seconds after your last page or action in the game through the game proxy (the game's live channel does not count). Start it again when you need it.
  • Waiting while you play (General.YieldWhenYouPlay) sees only queue changes: builds, research, shipyard orders, cancels and speed-ups. Sending a fleet by hand is not seen. TGoat recognises a kind of change once its own orders have produced it, so the wait may not work until then; a kind it has not seen yet is written once in the log. It needs the game's channel: without it, the bot plays on as before.
  • A sleep you asked for (/sleep, Sleep for…), a planned ghost sleep and a running break end when TGoat restarts or when you disable the instance. A new connection to the game keeps them.
  • Directives: TGoat does only two missions itself (see its settings). The others are done by its features as they play, or left to you: alliance, buddy, renaming, class, merchant.
  • Rewards event tasks and Project Orion missions are not handled yet. TGoat only reads past Orion and tells you when a reward waits; it never scans, sends fleets to anomalies or claims. It protects only fleets on your planets and moons: for a fleet at an anomaly, a hostile fleet gets a notice, no fleet-save.
  • Espionage from the galaxy view's button (AutoFarm's first espionage of a target) has no dispatch-page check, so the game's outlaw warning is not read there: TGoat relies on the galaxy view's mark for a much stronger player (s), which AutoFarm may have read up to AutoFarm.DaysToKeepOldSystemData days earlier. Every send from the fleet page, attacks included, is checked (General.AllowOutlawAttacks).
  • Renaming an instance while it runs empties the AutoFarm page of the lines written under the old name. New lines fill it again. The old ones stay on the Logs page and in the files.
  • Discord bot:
    • pending confirmations are lost when TGoat restarts. Run the command again.
    • a long Discord outage queues up to 100 notifications per instance. When the queue is full, the oldest routine notification is dropped, with a warning; attack alerts are kept.
    • the bot serves one server and takes no command in direct messages. Use a channel on that server.
  • Expedition results: the daily total in the log lives in memory, and a restart forgets it. What the game's messages say is kept 7 days.
  • Expedition messages have not been seen in OGame 13 yet. TGoat reads them with lenient names; a result it does not recognise is kept and named in the log, not counted.
  • Lifeforms found by an exploration have not been seen yet either, so the "species" count of the Discovery report is not confirmed.
  • Moons on the Map show their stock but not their storage capacity yet.
  • Harvest after an attack relies on the attack list. With the Defender off and nothing being sent, an attack on your celestial can go unseen.
  • The Discovery budget counts only the missions TGoat sent, not yours.
  • The Commander's build queue: the Brain still starts one build at a time.

Still to be checked in the game

Real play has not confirmed these yet: the image captcha, the sign-in from another computer, an update from a real release, several formulas and game rules, what lifeforms, expeditions and Discovery read in the game, AutoFarm's report reading, and the Discord bot on a real server. If one misbehaves for you, report it.

Everything still to be checked (23)
  • A full login with the image captcha solved by the built-in solver.
  • Assisted login (a browser's token with TGoat's simulated device was accepted in the game, November 2026):
    • a refused token really gets HTTP 401 or 403;
    • how long a token lives (the browser cookie lasts 90 days when you stay signed in).
  • The corrected formulas: the General's fuel reduction, crystal's position bonus, lifeform costs and durations.
  • The prerequisites of Nano Repair Bots, and five lifeform costs on which the sources disagree. TGoat keeps the highest.
  • Lifeforms, on an account that has one:
    • the production section of the bonuses page, seen only at 0%;
    • the class bonuses of the bonuses page with a lifeform boost: seen only unboosted. TGoat uses the boosted value the page shows;
    • the "bonus already maxed out" mark on a building or research button, seen only in the game's script. TGoat reads a positive number or "true", as the game does;
    • the population that keeps the six tier-1 research slots open. TGoat uses 1.55 million, an upper bound;
    • the building states "disabled" and "active", and open research slots at level 0;
    • the bonus per level and its cap, from the server data. TGoat uses a table from 13 November;
    • the gain of each production research and of the Collector enhancement, which TGoat uses to choose research. It comes from the game's descriptions;
    • the game's answer when a research slot's technology is chosen. TGoat trusts the research page read again.
  • Expedition size: whether the pathfinder doubles finds only when aboard.
  • Expedition results: how soon after the holding time an early return can come home. A fleet seen holding, then gone, is called lost only if it vanished before it could have flown home; that bound is a guess.
  • The offer of the day:
    • the page: price, rarity and renewal countdown;
    • the game's answer when the item is taken: another offer, or the text that none is left. TGoat takes that text to mean the next offer comes after the server's midnight, unless it gives a wait. When the game says nothing, TGoat tries again 24 hours later;
    • paying with honour points: the rate (100 units of metal per point on the page) and the honour total, read on the trader page.
  • Discovery:
    • the game's refusal texts in the galaxy view, and the mission cost, taken as 5,000 metal, 1,000 crystal and 500 deuterium;
    • how long before an explored position can be explored again. A wait text on every position would make TGoat skip that origin for a pass;
    • a refusal when sending is recognised by its English words only;
    • the game's answer when a lifeform is chosen. TGoat trusts only the Lifeforms page read again: the one answer seen so far was "An error has occured!".
  • The debris of destroyed satellites and crawlers, taken as 30%. The game does not give TGoat your universe's rate.
  • Game rules TGoat assumes:
    • the research lab cannot be raised while a research runs;
    • a colony ship that arrives while a colony awaits its decision finds the account full and flies back;
    • the arrival gate recharges like the departure gate, which TGoat reads.
  • The page readers for OGame v13. The tests use pages from older versions whose code is unchanged.
  • Abandoning a planet: that it disappears at once from the overview. A session-token login has no password, so this cannot be tested with it. The recall and jump gate confirmations were checked in the game on 14 November 2026.
  • AutoFarm:
    • whether real reports contain the defender's lifeform bonuses (without them, a target with satellites is refused with combat_defender_data_missing);
    • that the galaxy view gives the probe count of your game options on every server (without it, AutoFarm spies with 1 probe, the game's default, and warns once);
    • the newbie and strong-player protection flags of the galaxy view, and banned players (skipped to be safe);
    • the fuel of a recalled attack, counted as lost;
    • deleting several reports in one request: so far it was seen with one report only.
  • Sign in from another computer (tgoat login --to): tested with fakes, not yet run against a TGoat on a real server.
  • Updates: checking, downloading, installing and rolling back were tested against a simulated GitHub. The first real update will be the next alpha.
  • The night watch over a whole night. The channel's alert on a spy report and its health check were checked in the game in short tests.
  • The game's queue signals by day: which kinds of change the game sends for a build, a cancel, a speed-up or a shipyard order, whether TGoat's own orders send one, and which queue box each names. Waiting while you play and an early end of a build rely on them.
  • Project Orion in the event list: how it shows a hostile fleet on your fleet at an anomaly. TGoat decides by the destination alone.
  • Signals on OGame 13 pages: the number of Directives rewards on a full page, and the attack alert while a fleet is really on its way. If full pages do not carry the number, Directives finds new rewards only at its daily full read.
  • The game's outlaw warning before an attack or espionage on a much stronger player: TGoat reads it from the dispatch page's target check (targetIsStrong) as the game's own page script does, but no real answer with it set has been captured yet.
  • Galaxy view flags seen only at rest on the captured pages: the selected planet under attack, the latest espionage report on a planet, an outlaw player or account, and the phalanx in range. TGoat reads them as the game's own page script does.
  • The Discord bot: its tests run against a simulated Discord. A first run on a real server should confirm the slash commands and their suggestions, the buttons, the invite link, and the mention that rings on a phone.
