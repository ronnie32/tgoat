Pace and slots
Pauses between dispatches0 to 5 s between probes, none between attacks.Variable pauses with occasional longer breaks; variable batch size. Probe building: 10 minutes at most.

Choice of origin. The lowest score wins: flight time × (1 + 0.5 × load), load being the share of the origin's cargo ships (after AutoFarm.MinCargosToKeep) in flight after the send. An origin over 1.5 times slower than another capable one is never chosen.

Combat ships for defended targets (AutoFarm.BuildBreakers, Suggest by default). TBot never built combat ships. When AutoFarm turns a target down for its defences or ships, TGoat looks for the cheapest fleet your origin can build that would win, and the other turned-down targets near it that the same fleet wins. It sends you a proposal: what to build and where, its cost, the targets it opens, the loot per day, fuel, average losses, the payback in days and the risk. Accept it and TGoat orders the ships.

  • Raided regularly, a target gives up about its whole production each day, so that is the loot per day. The production is measured between two espionage reports at least an hour apart, or estimated from the mine levels and the energy the report shows. A target whose storage is full and whose buildings are hidden adds nothing to the payback. The stock taken by the first raids is mentioned, not counted in the payback.
  • TGoat proposes the smallest fleet that passes the last check before a raid; it adds escort only when that check needs it.
  • Nothing is proposed above AutoFarm.BreakerMaxPaybackDays (3 days), or when the Brain's reserves on that planet cannot pay.
  • The search accepts losing up to a tenth of the loot in a won battle; your raid settings stay as they are. The proposal says which settings AutoFarm needs to raid with the fleet, for example the combat planner with a higher MaxLossPercent.
  • Combat ships attract raiders. The proposal reminds you to keep the Defender's fleet-save on. Players inactive for 28 days or more may be deleted within about a week; the proposal says when it counts on them.
  • Automatic builds by itself, within the Brain's reserves, one order per pass, and only when your settings would raid with the fleet. Off does nothing.

Splitting big raids (off by default). With AutoFarm.SplitAcrossOrigins.Active, a target with no defence or fleet, whose loot no single origin can carry, is raided from up to AutoFarm.SplitAcrossOrigins.MaxOrigins origins, one slot each, each with at least AutoFarm.MinCargosToSend cargo ships. No effect with the combat planner on.
